Rumpus Golf · Golden thread · v1

Turn any floor into a mini golf course.

Every screen from boot to final scorecard, in play order. Designed at 1920×1080 for a laptop or a TV across the room: HUD type never drops below 28px, every action has a mouse target, a keycap and a gamepad button.

Player colors = ball colors. Green is excluded (matches the cup), red is reserved for the interface.
Assumptions
Up to 4 players, 1–9 holes; each hole is one of three course templates.
Play area default 3.0 × 2.0 m (a cleared living-room rug). Resizable in setup.
Camera feed prints in black and white; only zones, balls and players carry color, so tracking state is always the loudest thing on screen.
Stroke cap {{ strokeCap }} (configurable). Out of bounds = +1 and replace at the exit point.
Flat, zero-radius, 2px rules, flush-left labels — the Modernist system, with the ball palette as the only chaos.

Turn-change rules the UI relies on
Stroke = ball goes stopped → moving. Turn ends when it stops (2 cm / 0.5 s), then S12 wipes to the next player.
Holed = stopped inside the hole zone (v2 also checks height). Rolling through or bouncing off never counts.
Undo pops the last event (stroke, OOB, hole-out) within the current hole; Fix score edits totals directly and is logged.
Lost ball never blocks play; ambiguous re-appearance of two balls pauses to S09-style re-assignment.
